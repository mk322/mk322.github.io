01 / THE IDEA

Reasoning is computation.
Text is one way to express it.

A continuous thought can carry information before the model commits to words. That opens three connected questions: what should the thought contain, how should we generate it, and how should it improve with experience?

01

Learn representations around what the task needs next. Connect visual evidence, retrieved knowledge, and other modalities to reasoning.
