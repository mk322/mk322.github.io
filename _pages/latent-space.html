A research thread on continuous thoughts

Reasoning beyond words.

Language lets a model explain its reasoning. But must every intermediate thought become a word? We study how models can reason in continuous latent states: how to learn useful thoughts, generate possible next steps, and improve them through feedback.

From generating and refining thoughts to learning from feedback and reasoning across modalities.

About this research thread

A latent state can carry information before a model commits to words. Learning such states raises a connected set of questions: what information should they preserve, how can we generate a distribution of possible thoughts, and how can feedback improve the reasoning process?

The LaDiR series follows these questions from diffusion-based text reasoning to reinforcement learning and multimodal unification. NF-CoT explores probabilistic thoughts with normalizing flows. Scaffolding Minds learns visual targets and latent exploration together. Related work connects this direction to compressed knowledge and repeated internal computation.

This site brings together work by Murray Kang, Nikki Lijing Kuang, Yizhe Zhang, Yian Ma, Lianhui Qin, and their collaborators. Each paper credits its complete author list.
